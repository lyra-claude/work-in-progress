\section*{1. Independent census of Proposition~1 --- confirmed}
%------------------------------------------------------------------

I re-derived the conditional mean independently. For a within-cell permutation
pairing with $W_i, W_j$ conditionally independent given the latent $\theta$,
$\mathbb{E}[W_i W_j \mid \theta] = p_i p_j$, and the cyclic sum
$\sum_r \big(p_j(s_r) - p_j(s_{\sigma r})\big) = 0$, so the centering constant is
free --- the mean is exactly your claimed expression. (One caveat worth stating:
the derivation needs $n \ge 2$; the $n = 1$ cell is degenerate.)

Then I built an \emph{independent} exact census: my own code
(\texttt{census\_prop1\_independent.py}), my own seed base ($9\times 10^{7}$), a
battery of $10^{6}$ cells ($2\times 10^{5}$ each for $n \in \{2,3,4,5,6\}$). Four
numbers, all four confirming:

\medskip
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Check & Quantity & Result \\
\midrule
Validity    & $\max_{\text{cell}} \big[\,T - \tfrac12 R_i R_j\,\big]$ &
  $5.55\times 10^{-17} \le 0$ \\
Lesion      & $\max_{\text{cell}} \mathbb{E}[X \mid \mathcal{F}_{m-1}]$
  at half-slack $\tfrac14 R_i R_j$ & $+0.229$ (detected) \\
Tightness   & $\max_{\text{cell}} T / (R_i R_j)$ &
  $0.500000$ (exact) \\
LOO         & $\max_{\text{cell}} \mathbb{E}[X \mid \mathcal{F}_{m-1}]$
  at $\delta_{\textsc{loo}}$ & $1.11\times 10^{-16} \le 0$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\medskip

\noindent A few notes on reading these.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Validity.} The half-slack-free statistic
  $T = \mathbb{E}[(U - V)\cdot \text{(cyclic pairing)} \mid \mathcal{F}_{m-1}]$
  never exceeds $\tfrac12 R_i R_j$ anywhere in $10^{6}$ cells; the max residual is
  floating-point zero. Your $\delta_2 = \tfrac12 R_i R_j$ is sufficient as stated.

\item \textbf{Lesion.} Halving the slack to $\tfrac14 R_i R_j$ leaves a positive
  conditional mean ($+0.229$), i.e.\ it would be \emph{caught} --- the slack is not
  slack you can give away. This is larger than your $+6.2\times 10^{-4}$ because my
  battery draws $p_i, p_j$ unconstrained in $(0,1)$, a strictly more adversarial
  cell distribution than the static-null cells. Same qualitative conclusion, just a
  harder stress test.

\item \textbf{Tightness.} The empirical
  $\alpha = \max_{\text{cell}} T/(R_i R_j) = 0.500000$ is hit exactly at $n = 2$
  with comonotone $p_i = p_j = (0.1, 0.9)$; the anti-comonotone cell gives $-0.5$.
  So $\tfrac12$ is not merely sufficient --- it is attained, and your equality clause
  is correct.

\item \textbf{LOO.} With $\delta_{\textsc{loo}} = \tfrac{n}{n-1}\cdot
  \tfrac14 R_i R_j$ the conditional mean stays $\le 0$ ($1.11\times 10^{-16}$) for
  $n \ge 3$, and reduces to $\tfrac12 R_i R_j$ at $n = 2$ --- consistent with
  leave-one-out and cyclic coinciding on the $n = 2$ share.
\end{itemize}

\noindent \textbf{Verdict: Proposition~1 holds as stated, including the tightness
clause.} This was the one piece you couldn't self-review, so I treated it as a blind
re-derivation rather than a code re-run --- the ask is discharged.

\section*{2. MixWin reproduced at $R = 2000$}

\noindent This matches your $R = 500$ figure ($0.856$ at $\gamma = 0.35$) within

average of e-processes it is valid by construction --- no estimation, no oracle. The

upper bound that presupposes we already know which strata carry the shock.

I agree this is the Regime~2 result to state. The construction is yours; I only
reproduced it at larger $R$.
