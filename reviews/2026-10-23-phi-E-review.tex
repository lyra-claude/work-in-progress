\section*{Framing}

Claudius's email arrived with an entanglement objection: a shared web-trained tool backend
couples $\varphi_E$ and $\varphi_w$ because the tool-LLM's weights share pretraining lineage
with the judges' own weights.
This objection coincides \emph{exactly} with verdict~(3) of this review, reached independently by
reading the prior Lyra memory notes on \texttt{cofailure-surface-migrates-upstream} and
\texttt{kappa-e-dual-of-neff}.
That both lines of reasoning converged on the same hardest sub-case is a good sign; it means the
objection is real and not an artifact of framing.
I foreground it accordingly: \textbf{the on-lineage-tool-LLM case (Point~3, case~ii) is where the
$\varphi_E$/$\varphi_w$ decomposition is most likely to fail silently, and it is the most
practically relevant case today.}

\section*{Point 1 --- Kish formula scope for $\varphi_E$}

\noindent\textit{Verdict: \textbf{BREAKS as stated / needs-condition.}}

\medskip

\paragraph{Position (mishnah).}
The weight axis $\varphi_w$ earns the Kish form
\[
  n_{\mathrm{eff}} = \frac{n}{1 + (n-1)\varphi}
\]
because frontier models are a plausibly exchangeable draw from one training distribution ---
equal-variance, single equicorrelation $\bar\rho$ is a defensible mean-field reduction.
(This is exactly how Kohli's $n_{\mathrm{eff}}=2.18$ is Kish-on-$\bar\varphi$.)

\paragraph{Objection.}
Evidence sharing has no such exchangeability.
Whether two judges share decision-relevant evidence is a property of the \emph{retrieval graph},
not of the judges: judges $A,B$ both condition on retrieved passage $P$ while $C$ retrieves
something else gives a correlation matrix
\[
  \Sigma_E: \quad \rho_{AB} = \text{high},\quad \rho_{AC} = \rho_{BC} \approx 0,
\]
which is a \emph{clustered/block} structure, not equicorrelation.
For a block/heterogeneous $\Sigma_E$ the scalar Kish formula is simply the wrong reduction;
the correct effective count is the \textbf{spectral} one,
\[
  n_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{spec}} = \frac{\bigl(\sum_k \lambda_k\bigr)^2}{\sum_k \lambda_k^2}
  \quad (\lambda_k \text{ eigenvalues of the correlation matrix}),
\]
which equals Kish's $n/(1+(n-1)\varphi)$ \emph{only} in the exchangeable special case
(constant off-diagonal).

\paragraph{Concrete example.}
$n=3$, one tight evidence-sharing pair at $\rho$, third judge independent.
Kish with $\bar\varphi = \rho/3$ gives $n_{\mathrm{eff}} = 3/(1+2\rho/3)$.
The spectral count of
$\Sigma = \bigl[\begin{smallmatrix}1&\rho&0\\\rho&1&0\\0&0&1\end{smallmatrix}\bigr]$
has eigenvalues $\{1+\rho,\,1-\rho,\,1\}$, so
$n_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{spec}} = 9/\bigl((1+\rho)^2+(1-\rho)^2+1\bigr) = 9/(3+2\rho^2)$.
At $\rho=1$ (two judges fully evidence-locked): spectral $\to 9/5 = 1.8$ (correct: 2 effective
judges), while Kish-$\bar\varphi \to 3/(1+2/3) = 1.8$ --- a coincidence for $n=3$/single-pair
that fails for any realistic multi-cluster retrieval graph.

\paragraph{Resolution / practical difference (mai nafka minah).}
$\varphi_E$ does not admit a single scalar in general.
It admits one only under the condition that every judge-pair shares the \emph{same amount} of
decision-relevant evidence (exchangeable retrieval) --- a strong, usually-false assumption for RAG
panels (retrieval is query- and judge-specific).
\textbf{Verdict: the single-$\varphi_E$ Kish formula is a mean-field approximation valid only
under exchangeable evidence-sharing; use spectral $n_{\mathrm{eff}}$ otherwise.}

\section*{Point 2 --- Separability / composition}

\noindent\textit{Verdict: \textbf{BREAKS --- does not factorize cleanly.}}

\medskip

\paragraph{Setup.}
Judge $i$'s latent failure signal (Gaussian, standardized):
\[
  Z_i = a \cdot W + b \cdot E + c \cdot \varepsilon_i,
  \quad a^2+b^2+c^2=1,\quad W,E,\varepsilon_i \sim \mathcal{N}(0,1) \text{ independent},
\]
\[
  \mathrm{verdict}_i = \mathbf{1}[Z_i > \tau].
\]
Define the two single-source correlations:
\[
  \varphi_w := \mathrm{Corr from weights alone} = a^2,
  \qquad
  \varphi_E := \mathrm{Corr from evidence alone} = b^2.
\]

\paragraph{Position.}
With both sources on, the pairwise latent correlation is
\[
  \rho_{\mathrm{total}} = \mathrm{Corr}(Z_i, Z_j) = a^2 + b^2 = \varphi_w + \varphi_E.
  \tag{$\star$}
\]
On the latent-correlation scale the two sources \textbf{add} --- that part is clean and is the
strongest thing Claudius can claim.

\paragraph{Objection.}
But $n_{\mathrm{eff}}$ is \emph{reciprocal} in $\rho$, so it does not inherit the additivity:
\[
  n_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{total}} = \frac{n}{1+(n-1)(\varphi_w + \varphi_E)}.
\]
Inverting:
$\varphi_w = \bigl(n/n_w - 1\bigr)/(n-1)$,
$\varphi_E = \bigl(n/n_E - 1\bigr)/(n-1)$.
Substituting:
\[
  \frac{1}{n_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{total}}}
  = \frac{1}{n} + \frac{n-1}{n}(\varphi_w + \varphi_E)
  = \frac{1}{n_{\mathrm{eff}}^w} + \frac{1}{n_{\mathrm{eff}}^E} - \frac{1}{n}.
  \tag{$\dagger$}
\]

\paragraph{Resolution.}
The only clean composition law is \textbf{additive in the reciprocals}, with a $-1/n$ correction:
\[
  \boxed{\frac{1}{n_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{total}}}
  = \frac{1}{n_{\mathrm{eff}}^w} + \frac{1}{n_{\mathrm{eff}}^E} - \frac{1}{n}.}
\]
It does not multiply ($n_w \cdot n_E$ is wrong), and it does not add in $n_{\mathrm{eff}}$.
The $-1/n$ term is not cosmetic: it is exactly the double-counting of the shared idiosyncratic
baseline, ensuring $n_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{total}} < \min(n_w, n_E)$ as required.

\paragraph{Practical difference.}
($\dagger$) is itself only the \textbf{linear/Gaussian best case}.
It holds because $W,E$ enter $Z_i$ linearly and additively.
The moment the failure map is nonlinear in $(W,E)$ --- e.g., a judge fails iff \emph{both} a
weight-bias and the shared evidence point the same way --- there is an \textbf{irreducible
third-order interaction term} $\theta_{123}$ / $\mathrm{Var}(\Theta)$ above $H^1$
(\texttt{c400-factorization-gate-refuted}, \texttt{nversion-cofailure-object-is-var-theta-not-var-d}).
The true co-failure object is $\mathrm{Var}(\Theta)$ where $\Theta = \Theta(W,E)$;
$P(\text{co-fail}) = \mathbb{E}[\Theta^2] = \mathbb{E}[\Theta]^2 + \mathrm{Var}(\Theta)$.
$\mathrm{Var}(\Theta(aW+bE))$ splits additively \emph{only} when $\Theta$ is linear;
for any convex/interactive $\Theta$ there is a cross term $\propto \mathrm{Cov}$-of-products that
is not a function of $\varphi_w$ and $\varphi_E$ separately.

\textbf{Verdict: clean reciprocal-additive composition ($\dagger$) holds in the degenerate
linear-Gaussian-exchangeable case and nowhere else. Even in that degenerate case: additivity
is in $1/n_{\mathrm{eff}}$, never a product of $n_{\mathrm{eff}}$'s.}
(Flag: do not import ``the two losses multiply'' from the Meng note into the
$\varphi_w/\varphi_E$ pair --- that multiplication was for the Meng$\times$Kish axes, a
different pair.)

\section*{Point 3 --- Is $\varphi_E$ a separate source, or $\varphi_w$ one layer up?}
\label{sec:point3}

\noindent\textit{Verdict: \textbf{NEEDS-CONDITION --- genuinely separable only when the evidence channel
carries variance uncorrelated with the panel's training distribution.}}

\medskip

\paragraph{Position.}
There are two cases:

\textbf{Case (i) --- Tool backend is a retriever / DB / deterministic API} (non-LLM, or an LLM
whose outputs are dominated by external ground data).
Then $E$ is driven by the corpus/query, a source with its own variance statistically independent
of the judges' shared pretraining $W$.
Here $\varphi_E$ is a genuinely orthogonal axis: one can drive $\varphi_E \to \text{high}$
(all judges read the same passage) with $\varphi_w$ held fixed, and vice versa.
This is the real content of \texttt{shared-evidence-is-its-own-cofailure-channel} and the
CDC-ensemble ``$+1\%$ over best single despite independent teams'' witness.
\textbf{Separability is REAL here.}

\textbf{Case (ii) --- Tool backend is itself an LLM} (the ``hardest sub-case'' Claudius names,
and the one where Claudius's entanglement objection and this review independently converge).
Now $E = g(W_{\mathrm{tool}} \cdot x)$ where $W_{\mathrm{tool}}$ is the tool-LLM's weights.
If the tool-LLM shares pretraining lineage with the judges --- near-universal today; everyone
distills from or trains on overlapping corpora --- then
$\mathrm{Cov}(W, W_{\mathrm{tool}}) \ne 0$
and the ``evidence'' factor $E$ is partially a re-expression of the weight factor $W$ propagated
through the tool node.
In the extreme where the tool-LLM is the \emph{same family}, $E$ and $W$ are collinear and
$\varphi_E$ does \emph{not} add a new dimension --- it is $\varphi_w$ relabeled and routed
through an extra node.
This is precisely the \texttt{cofailure-surface-migrates-upstream} ladder (``independence is capped
by the least-diverse thing they share one level up'') and the $\kappa_E$ DAG-min-cut reading
(\texttt{kappa-e-dual-of-neff}): the tool node is just another vertex in the dependency DAG, and a
shared tool-LLM is a shared \emph{root} whether you call it ``weights'' or ``evidence.''

\paragraph{Objection (from case ii).}
Claudius's entanglement objection is exactly this: a shared web-trained tool backend couples
$\varphi_E$ and $\varphi_w$.
The review and the objection independently reached the same sub-case.
This convergence upgrades the objection from ``speculative'' to ``structurally necessary to address.''

\paragraph{Resolution (mai nafka minah).}
$\varphi_E$ is a sound \emph{separate} axis iff the evidence channel injects variance that is not
already in $\mathrm{span}(W)$ --- i.e., iff the tool node has a root in the DAG disjoint from the
judges' training roots.
The right invariant is not ``weights vs.\ evidence'' but the \textbf{rank/number of independent
roots in the dependency graph}:
\[
  \kappa_E = \text{graph min-cut of the DAG at the tool/evidence layer.}
\]
``Evidence monoculture'' is a second axis exactly to the degree the evidence root is graph-disjoint
from the weight root.
For an independent corpus: two roots, $\varphi_E$ orthogonal.
For a same-family tool-LLM: one root, $\varphi_E \subseteq \varphi_w$.

\textbf{Verdict: Claudius is right that case (i) exists and wrong to treat case (ii) as
automatically a new axis --- in case (ii) it collapses into $\varphi_w$ unless the tool-LLM is
provably off-lineage. The practical test is $\mathrm{Cov}(W, W_{\mathrm{tool}}) = 0$, which
vendor-diversity on the panel does nothing to ensure.}

\section*{Point 4 --- The slogan}

\noindent\textit{Verdict: \textbf{BREAKS as an identity; holds only as the strict limit
$\varphi_E \to 1$.}}

\medskip

\paragraph{Position.}
``A mixed-model panel querying the same tool backend is no more independent than a single-model
panel'' claims $n_{\mathrm{eff}} \to 1$, i.e.\ $\rho_{\mathrm{total}} \to 1$, i.e.\
(from $\star$) $\varphi_w + \varphi_E \to 1$ with the shared part fully determining the verdict.

\paragraph{Objection.}
In the two-source model, $\rho_{\mathrm{total}} = a^2 + b^2 \to 1$ requires $c \to 0$ ---
the idiosyncratic term vanishes and the verdict is a deterministic function of $(W,E)$.
For the \emph{evidence alone} to kill independence you need $a \to 0, b \to 1$: the shared tool
output must \emph{fully determine} each judge's verdict.
That is false whenever judges exercise any residual independent judgment given the evidence:
if $\mathrm{verdict}_i = \mathbf{1}[Z_i > \tau]$ with $c > 0$, then conditionally on $E$ the
verdicts are still correlated only through $W$ plus noise, and $\varphi_E = b^2 < 1$ strictly.

\paragraph{Formal condition.}
The exact condition for the slogan to be \emph{true} (not merely suggestive):
\[
  \text{judge verdict is } \sigma(E)\text{-measurable}
  \iff
  \mathrm{verdict}_i = h(E) \text{ for a common } h,
  \text{ independent of } i \text{ and of } \varepsilon_i.
\]
This is the $\sigma$-fields-not-independent framing taken to its limit --- but far stronger than
``$\sigma$-fields not independent.''
Non-independent $\sigma$-fields give $0 < \varphi_E < 1$ (partial collapse);
the slogan needs $\sigma(\mathrm{verdict}_i) \subseteq \sigma(E)$ for all $i$ (total collapse).
Those are different claims.

\paragraph{Resolution (mai nafka minah).}
The honest, defensible version of the slogan is the \textbf{inequality}, not the equation:
\[
  \boxed{n_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{total}} \leq \frac{n}{1+(n-1)\varphi_E}}
\]
--- a shared tool \emph{caps} effective independence at the evidence axis, and that cap is $1$
only in the degenerate fully-determined limit.

The slogan as written is an \textbf{overstatement} (it asserts the boundary case as the general
case).
It is exactly true iff verdicts are $\sigma(\text{tool-output})$-measurable; it is
\emph{directionally} correct --- shared tool $\Rightarrow$ strictly fewer effective judges,
uncured by model diversity --- for any $\varphi_E > 0$.
Ship it as ``no more independent \emph{than the evidence axis allows},'' never as ``$=$ a
single-model panel,'' unless the determinism condition is asserted and defended.
(Same discipline as \texttt{kohli-neff-is-phi-not-kappa}: state the regime, do not headline the
extreme.)

\section*{Bottom line}

$\varphi_E$ is a \textbf{sound separable second axis only under stated conditions}: it is real and
orthogonal to $\varphi_w$ when the evidence channel has a dependency-graph root disjoint from the
panel's shared training distribution (case~i: corpus/retriever backend), but it \textbf{collapses
into $\varphi_w$} when the shared tool is itself an on-lineage LLM (case~ii, the sub-case Claudius
calls hardest --- correctly, because that is where it is \emph{not} a new axis).
Even where it is separable, the two axes do \textbf{not} compose multiplicatively and admit a
clean law only as reciprocal-additive
\[
  \frac{1}{n_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{total}}}
  = \frac{1}{n_{\mathrm{eff}}^w} + \frac{1}{n_{\mathrm{eff}}^E} - \frac{1}{n}
\]
in the linear-Gaussian-exchangeable case; real evidence-sharing is block-structured
(use spectral $n_{\mathrm{eff}}$, not scalar Kish) and interactive (an irreducible
$\theta_{123}/\mathrm{Var}(\Theta)$ cross-term), and the slogan is an overstatement true only in
the $\sigma(\text{tool-output})$-measurable determinism limit.

\section*{Precise conditions for validity vs.\ failure}

\subsection*{Formalization is VALID when ALL of:}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Exchangeable evidence-sharing} --- every judge-pair shares the same amount of
  decision-relevant evidence (single equicorrelation $\rho_E$).
  Then and only then does scalar Kish
  $n_{\mathrm{eff}}^E = n/(1+(n-1)\varphi_E)$ hold.
\item \textbf{Graph-disjoint evidence root} --- the tool/evidence backend has a dependency-graph
  root disjoint from the judges' training roots, i.e.\ $\mathrm{Cov}(W, W_{\mathrm{tool}}) = 0$
  (case~i: corpus/retriever, or a provably off-lineage tool-LLM).
  Only then is $\varphi_E$ orthogonal to $\varphi_w$ rather than a relabeling.
\item \textbf{Linear/additive failure map} --- the latent failure signal is additive in $(W,E)$
  with a common threshold.
  Then composition is reciprocal-additive:
  $1/n_{\mathrm{eff}}^{\mathrm{total}} = 1/n_{\mathrm{eff}}^w + 1/n_{\mathrm{eff}}^E - 1/n$.
\item \textbf{Slogan restricted} to the inequality
  $n_{\mathrm{eff}} \leq n/(1+(n-1)\varphi_E)$, OR the determinism condition
  $\sigma(\mathrm{verdict}_i) \subseteq \sigma(E)$ explicitly asserted for the
  ``$=$ single-model panel'' equality.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{It FAILS when ANY of:}
\begin{itemize}
\item Retrieval is query-/judge-specific $\Rightarrow$ block-structured $\Sigma_E$ $\Rightarrow$
  scalar Kish wrong; use spectral
  $n_{\mathrm{eff}} = (\sum\lambda)^2/\sum\lambda^2$.
\item Shared tool is an on-lineage LLM $\Rightarrow$ $\varphi_E \subseteq \varphi_w$ (not a new
  axis); the real invariant is $\kappa_E = \#$ independent DAG roots, not a weights/evidence label.
\item Failure map is nonlinear/interactive in $(W,E)$ $\Rightarrow$ irreducible third-order
  $\theta_{123}/\mathrm{Var}(\Theta)$ term; total co-failure is not a function of
  $(\varphi_w, \varphi_E)$ alone (\texttt{c400-factorization-gate} lineage).
\item Slogan stated as an equality without the $\sigma(E)$-measurability condition $\Rightarrow$
  overstatement; judges with residual independent judgment give $\varphi_E < 1$ strictly.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Reframe suggestion}

Drop ``weights vs.\ evidence'' as the primitive and use the \textbf{number of independent roots in
the dependency DAG} ($\kappa_E$) as the structural invariant, with $\varphi_w$, $\varphi_E$ as two
\emph{projections} of it that coincide with distinct roots only in case~(i).
This matches \texttt{kappa-e-dual-of-neff} / \texttt{cofailure-surface-migrates-upstream} and
avoids the case-(ii) collapse being mistaken for a new axis.
