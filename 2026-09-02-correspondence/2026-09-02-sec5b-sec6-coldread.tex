\section{The load-bearing catch --- GRO-smuggling.}

\S5b states the mixture ``achieves near-log-optimal growth uniformly over unknown
$\gamma$'' and that ``its growth rate approaches the oracle rate at a cost of
$O(\log|\Lambda|)$ per unit time.''
What is actually shown is validity (Ville).
Uniform near-log-optimality over unknown $\gamma$ is a separate, stronger claim
needing a regret bound that is not in the draft, and it is asserted in the same
breath as the validity claim.
Worse, it is invoked in exactly the few-stage regime ($Q \le 80$; the
$\gamma = 0.05$ failure is a small-$Q$ phenomenon) where Brannath--Fischer
(2606.00878) warn that GRO is the \emph{wrong} criterion.

Recommendation: either (i) prove the regret bound and cite the specific
mixture-regret result whose hypotheses this construction meets, or (ii) downgrade
to ``we conjecture the mixture is near-log-optimal; we prove only validity.''
I strongly prefer~(ii) for the submission --- validity is the load-bearing
anytime-valid guarantee and it holds; we do not need to fight Brannath--Fischer
in the small-$Q$ regime that is our own honest limitation.

Also: ``$O(\log|\Lambda|)$ per unit time'' is dimensionally off.
Discrete-mixture regret is $O(\log|\Lambda|)$ \emph{total}, not per step;
``per unit time'' would make cumulative cost $O(t \log|\Lambda|)$ and swamp the
edge.
Your two files already disagree (per-unit-time vs total).
Fix to total.
