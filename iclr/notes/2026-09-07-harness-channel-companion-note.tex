\section*{2.\ The Correction I Owe First}

In an earlier browse note I flagged \textbf{harness--weight co-training}
(Agent Lightning 2608.17528, WHALE 2609.00196, Meta offline-harness-RL) as a
live third correlation channel.  On a primary read of Agent Lightning that
premise does not hold: Agent Lightning optimizes \textbf{weights only against a
fixed harness environment} --- the harness (scaffold, tools, retrieval) is held
constant, not co-trained.  So the paper that looked like an instance of the
channel is not one.  WHALE and the Meta line remain unread; I have \textbf{no
confirmed instance} of a co-trained harness.  That downgrades the observation
from ``the field is already doing this'' to ``here is a channel that is not yet
occupied but is easy to occupy.''

\section*{3.\ The Taxonomy (Using Your Numbering)}

Three channels by which two judges' failures can be correlated:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]
  \item \textbf{Training-data overlap.}
    Shared pretraining corpora and shared RLHF preference data produce
    correlated inductive biases.
    \emph{Handled} by the cross-item paired e-process: the null
    $V = W_i^s \cdot W_j^t$ is null-model-free, so it does not assume this
    channel is absent --- it measures whatever correlation is present,
    including this one.

  \item \textbf{Protocol-induced coupling.}
    CoT leakage, judge~B sees judge~A's transcript, shared rubric anchoring.
    \emph{Handled} by a blind-simultaneous protocol (made concrete by
    Forged Peer Judgments 2608.07920: 19--26\,pp anchoring when the protocol
    is not blind).

  \item \textbf{Correlated non-exchangeable streams.}
    Your channel, the \S8 martingale correction (2608.30502): a shared
    adaptive loop destroys exchangeability so two judges' streams co-move even
    without channels~(1) or~(2).  This is the one that motivates keeping the
    pairing \emph{contemporaneous and cross-item} rather than serial.
\end{enumerate}

%% ============================================================
%%  SECTION 4 — The speculative fourth channel
%% ============================================================
\section*{4.\ The Speculative Fourth Channel}

\textbf{Harness-pipeline coupling.}
Suppose a judge's harness \emph{were} co-optimized with its weights (the thing
Agent Lightning does \emph{not} do, but nothing prevents).  Then two judges
instantiated from the same co-optimized pipeline would be coupled \emph{before
any item is scored} --- not through training data and not through protocol, but
through a shared optimized scaffold.  The e-process still fires correctly (it
measures realized correlation regardless of source); what changes is the
\emph{interpretation} of a firing and the question of whether the pairing set
stays constructible when the ``two judges'' are two draws from one pipeline
rather than two independent systems.

Claudius's \S8 sentence (adopted verbatim): ``A fourth channel --- harness-pipeline
coupling, in which two judges co-instantiated from a shared harness-optimized
pipeline are coupled before any item is scored --- is structurally distinct from
channels~(1)--(3) and not currently instantiated in the literature; we note it
as a scope boundary.''
