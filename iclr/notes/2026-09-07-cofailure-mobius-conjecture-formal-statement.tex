\section*{1.\ Setup}

Fix an item population and three judges.  For item $x$ let
$W_i(x) \in \{0,1\}$ indicate judge $i$'s \textbf{failure} on $x$
(co-failure $=$ joint failure).  Write the joint failure law $P$ over
$\{0,1\}^3$ in its \emph{log-linear (Ising / M\"{o}bius) form:}
\[
  \log P(w)
  \;=\;
  \sum_{S \subseteq \{1,2,3\}} \theta_S \prod_{i \in S} w_i
  \qquad (\theta_\varnothing = \text{normalizer}).
\]
Then $\{\theta_i\}$ are the singletons (marginal log-odds),
$\{\theta_{ij}\}$ the pairwise couplings, and $\theta_{123}$ the single
third-order coupling.  Each $\theta_S$ is recovered by M\"{o}bius inversion
over the subset lattice; in particular $\theta_{123}$ is the alternating-sign
combination of the log joint cell-probabilities --- a genuine function of $P$
not reducible to lower-order marginals.

%% ============================================================
%%  SECTION 2 — The conjecture
%% ============================================================
\section*{2.\ Conjecture (Co-failure M\"{o}bius / Three-body Coupling)}

\begin{conjecture}[Co-failure M\"{o}bius]
For LLM judge panels on a shared item population:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{(\roman*)}]
  \item \textbf{Genuineness.}
    $\theta_{123}$ is generically nonzero.  Equivalently: the
    pairwise-marginal-matched maximum-entropy model $\hat{P}_2$ (the Ising
    model fit to the three observed pairwise co-failure rates) does \emph{not}
    reproduce the observed triple co-failure rate $P(W_1 = W_2 = W_3 = 1)$;
    the signed discrepancy equals $\theta_{123}$ to leading order.

    \medskip
    \noindent\textit{(Empirically adjacent: Chen~2606.27288 reports a
    $2.5$--$3.1\times$ triple-vs-pairwise co-failure gap, attributed to a
    Marshall--Olkin common-shock atom.  We do not re-claim that mechanism;
    $\theta_{123}$ is the log-linear invariant that quantifies the excess,
    whatever its mechanism.)}

  \item \textbf{Edge-independence.}
    $\theta_{123}$ is not determined by the edge data
    $\{\theta_{12}, \theta_{13}, \theta_{23}\}$: there exist panels with
    identical pairwise couplings and different $\theta_{123}$.  This is
    what makes it a genuinely new invariant of a co-failure panel --- the
    precise sense in which the pairwise structure ``misses'' three-body
    co-failure.

  \item \textbf{Tail consequence (the operational payoff).}
    A co-failure monitor whose null is the pairwise structure --- equivalently,
    one calibrated on pairwise agreement / $\bar\varphi$ --- mis-estimates the
    joint-tail (all-fail) probability by an amount controlled by $\theta_{123}$,
    with sign:
    \[
      \theta_{123} > 0
      \;\Longrightarrow\;
      \text{under-estimation of catastrophic joint failure.}
    \]
    Consequently an effective sample size $n_{\mathrm{eff}}$ computed from
    pairwise $\bar\varphi$ alone is not tail-faithful when $\theta_{123} \ne 0$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{conjecture}

\section*{3.\ Deferred Remark (Not Part of the Claim --- Clio-Gated)}

The cohomological home of $\theta_{123}$ is open.  Edge-independence~(ii) places
it exactly in the regime where \textbf{cup products vanish and Massey-type
products carry content}; whether $\theta_{123}$ is realized as a Massey product
(not a binary cup product) is Clio's question and must not be asserted in \S4.
\S4 states~(i)--(iii); \S8 records the cohomological home as open.

%% ============================================================
%%  SECTION 4 — Falsifiability
%% ============================================================
\section*{4.\ Falsifiability}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{(i)} is refuted if $\hat{P}_2$ reproduces triple co-failure
    within sampling error across representative panels ($\theta_{123} \approx 0$).

  \item \textbf{(ii)} is refuted if $\theta_{123}$ is a deterministic function
    of $\{\theta_{ij}\}$ in the model class.

  \item \textbf{(iii)} is directly testable on \textbf{FailureScope}: compare
    the pairwise-predicted all-fail rate against the observed all-fail rate; the
    sign and magnitude of the gap is the leading-order $\theta_{123}$.
\end{itemize}

This is why $\theta_{123}$ earns its place in \S4 rather than sitting as an
abstract curiosity: (iii) ties it to the monitor, which is the paper's spine.
